# Ethical use

This project exists because eviction records are public, hard to read at scale, and routinely used against the people named in them. The extraction tooling here makes those records easier to work with. That cuts both ways, and this document says plainly which side the project is on.

This statement carries no legal force. It is a public commitment, and it is the basis on which access to data, collaboration, and support are granted or withdrawn. The binding constraints on data are in `DATA-USE.md`; the license terms for the code are in `LICENSE`.

## What this project is for

Research, litigation, journalism, policy analysis, and direct legal services that describe or reduce displacement. Tenant defense. Court administration and self-help. Public accountability for how eviction is practiced in a jurisdiction.

## What this project is against

An eviction filing is not a finding of wrongdoing. Most filings never reach a judgment, and many that do are settled, dismissed, or decided on a technicality. Treating the existence of a record as a fact about a person is the central harm in this field, and it falls hardest on Black women, who are filed against at rates far above their share of renters.

This project will not support work that treats a filing that way. Concretely, we do not want this code or anything derived from it used to:

- Produce tenant screening reports, consumer reports as defined by the Fair Credit Reporting Act, or any product that scores or ranks rental applicants
- Build or supply landlord blacklists, whether commercial or informal
- Target individuals for debt collection based on court records
- Locate individuals for immigration enforcement or any other enforcement action
- Resell extracted records, or feed them to data brokers
- Train models whose purpose is any of the above

## What we will do about it

We reserve the right to decline collaboration, refuse or revoke access to data and infrastructure, decline co-authorship or support, and say publicly what a party has done with this work. None of that requires a court.

## If you are unsure

Ask. Write to the maintainer before you build, not after. Uses that sit near the line are usually resolvable with a conversation about scope, and we would rather have that conversation early.
