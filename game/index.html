How to judge a card

You are checking one thing: does the typed address below the picture say the same thing as the address in the picture?

  • Matches if they carry the same street number, street, unit, city and ZIP. Ignore differences of spacing, capitals and punctuation, and ignore ordinary abbreviations: WA and WASHINGTON are the same answer, as are St and Street, Apt and Apartment, S and South. A missing ZIP is only a mismatch if the picture shows one.
  • Does not match if anything meaningful differs, including a missing unit or apartment number, a missing or wrong direction such as SE against S, or a wrong digit.
  • Can't tell if the picture is too blurred or cut off to read. This is a useful answer, not a failure.

After you mark one wrong you can type what the picture actually says. That is optional, and worth extra points, because a correction teaches the extractor far more than a rejection does. Skip it whenever you would rather keep moving.

Some cards arrive with no typed address at all and ask you to write one. Those are filings the reader could not make out, usually because they were filled in by hand. There is no other way to get them into the data, so they are worth the most.

Black bars are deliberate. Names are covered because only the address needs checking.
